About Me

Creative Director & AI Visual Specialist

5+ Years in Creative Tech

I combine traditional design expertise with cutting-edge AI technology to create visual stories that capture attention and drive engagement in today's crowded digital landscape.

My journey began in traditional graphic design, where I honed my eye for composition, color theory, and visual storytelling. As AI tools like Midjourney and Runway emerged, I recognized their potential to transform creative work and dedicated myself to mastering these technologies.

What sets my approach apart is the balance between technological innovation and human creativity. I don't just prompt AI tools—I direct them with purpose, refining outputs through an iterative process until they achieve the perfect blend of originality and strategic alignment with your brand objectives.

Beyond surface-level AI image generation, I've developed advanced techniques that combine outputs from multiple AI systems—using Midjourney's compositional strength, enhancing details with DALL-E, and finalizing with traditional editing in Photoshop and Final Cut Pro to achieve results that stand apart from standard AI-generated content.
